# Mobile_Power-BI-Analytics_Dashboard

Welcome to the Mobile_Power-BI-Analytics-Dashboard repository! 🚀
This project demonstrates an interactive business intelligence and analytics solution built with Microsoft Power BI. The dashboard transforms mobile sales transaction data into actionable insights covering sales performance, product trends, customer ratings, payment methods, and geographic performance.

---

## 🚀 Project Overview

The **Mobile_Power-BI-Analytics-Dashboard** provides an interactive view of sales performance using a centralized `Sales data` dataset.

The dashboard enables users to analyze:

* Overall sales performance
* Transaction volume
* Quantity sold
* Average product price
* Brand performance
* Mobile model performance
* Payment method distribution
* Customer ratings
* Daily and monthly sales trends
* City-level sales performance

Interactive filters allow users to drill down into the data by **brand, mobile model, payment method, and date**.

---

## 📊 Dashboard Features

### 🔑 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard provides high-level business metrics through interactive KPI cards, including:

* **Total Sales** – Overall revenue generated from transactions
* **Total Quantity** – Total number of units sold
* **Total Transactions** – Number of sales transactions
* **Average Price** – Average selling price across products

These KPIs provide stakeholders with an immediate overview of business performance.

---

### 📈 Sales Trend Analysis

The dashboard includes time-based visualizations to understand sales patterns and identify trends.

Key analysis includes:

* Sales performance by day
* Monthly units sold
* Daily sales trends
* Time-based performance comparisons

These visualizations help identify periods of high and low sales activity.

---

### 📱 Product & Model Analysis

Product-level analysis is provided through visualizations that compare different mobile models and brands.

Users can analyze:

* Sales by mobile model
* Sales performance by brand
* Quantity sold by brand
* Transaction volume by brand
* Average product price

This helps identify the products and brands contributing most to overall sales.

---

### 💳 Payment Method Analysis

A dedicated payment-method visualization shows the distribution of transactions across different payment methods.

This allows businesses to understand:

* Most frequently used payment methods
* Transaction distribution
* Customer payment preferences

---

### ⭐ Customer Rating Analysis

The dashboard includes a **Customer Ratings** analysis using a funnel visualization.

This helps evaluate:

* Customer rating distribution
* Number of transactions associated with ratings
* Overall customer satisfaction patterns

The analysis can help identify areas where customer experience may be improved.

---

### 🌍 Geographic Sales Analysis

A map visualization provides city-level sales analysis.

Users can explore:

* Total sales by city
* Geographic sales distribution
* High-performing locations
* Regional sales patterns

This can support location-based business and marketing decisions.

---

## 🎛️ Interactive Filters

The dashboard provides several slicers that allow users to dynamically filter the analysis.

Available filters include:

* **Brand**
* **Mobile Model**
* **Payment Method**
* **Day**

These filters allow stakeholders to move from high-level performance analysis to more detailed product and transaction-level insights.

## 🧩 Data Model

The Power BI report is built around a centralized sales dataset named:

```xlsx / Tabular Sales Data```

The dataset contains business attributes and measures used throughout the dashboard, including:

### Dimensions / Attributes

* Date
* Brand
* Mobile Model
* Payment Method
* City
* Customer Ratings
* Day Name

### Measures / Metrics

* Total Sales
* Total Quantity
* Transactions
* Average Price

The model is designed to support interactive filtering and analytical reporting within Power BI.
